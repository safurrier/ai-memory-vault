## Topic MOCs

_Run `/personalize` to create seed MOCs for your interests, or create them manually with `type: moc` and `up: "[[Resources]]"`._

## How to use

- Create a MOC (Map of Content) for each major topic area
- Individual notes point `up:` to their topic MOC
- Topic MOCs point `up:` to Resources
- Use `/obsidian-read` to create notes from URLs, `/obsidian-organize` to add metadata
