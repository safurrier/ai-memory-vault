# The 5 Levels of Communicating Impact

**Purpose:** Helps engineers describe the impact of their work more effectively for performance reviews, resumes, and career growth by progressing from simple actions to business outcomes.

## The Levels

### Level 1: What you did
Plain description of the task or work completed.

> "Migrated the authentication system to OAuth 2.0."

### Level 2: Had impact
Shows an effect beyond just doing the task.

> "Reduced login failures."

### Level 3: Specific impact
Adds data with clear metrics.

> "Reduced login failures by 40% (from 15% to 9%)."

### Level 4: Tied to a goal
Connects the result to a team or company objective.

> "Exceeded Q2 reliability target by 2×."

### Level 5: Business-level impact
Links outcomes to financial or strategic value.

> "Prevented ~$2.1M annual revenue loss and saved 15 hours/week in support time."

---

## Quick Reference

| Level | Focus | Signal Word |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Action | "Did X" |
| 2 | Effect | "Improved/Reduced" |
| 3 | Metrics | "By X%" |
| 4 | Goal | "Achieved/Exceeded target" |
| 5 | Business | "$X revenue/cost" |

---

## Leveling Up Template

**Starting statement:** [What you did]

**Questions to level up:**
1. What changed as a result? (→ Level 2)
2. By how much? What are the numbers? (→ Level 3)
3. What goal or OKR does this support? (→ Level 4)
4. What's the dollar value or business metric impact? (→ Level 5)
